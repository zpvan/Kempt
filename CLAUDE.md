# Kempt Project Guide

## Process documents

- Do not commit files under `docs/superpowers/`.
- Superpowers specs, plans, and other process artifacts are local-only and must stay out of releases.
- Product and artifact names are `Kempt` and `kempt-server`; Chinese UI brand is `企理`.
- New data uses only `kempt.db`, `com.kempt.workflow`, `kempt.*` localStorage keys, and `kempt-full-backup` v3.
